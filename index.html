Baseline

  • 3 classes: pothole, crack, manhole

  • No train/val split — inflated metrics
  • Indentation bug: train_model nested inside create_yaml
  • No CPU/GPU auto-detection
⚙️ Hyperparameters
  • Optimizer: RAdam
  • LR0: 0.00761 · LRF: 0.0698
  • Batch: 16 · Workers: 4
  • Img size: 640 · Epochs: 100
  • No augmentation params set

⚠️ Caveats
  • Very small val/test sets (15 val, 12 test images)
  • High mAP may reflect tiny eval set, not real performance
  • mAP50 still rising at epoch 100 — training ended before convergence
  • Two code blocks merged — confusing structure

Training loss curves (box, cls, dfl) all drop steadily, confirming effective learning. A notable sharp spike at epoch ~80 in train losses and val losses is visible — this corresponds to the K-Fold cross-validation fold boundary where the model resets to a new train/val split. After the reset, losses recover quickly and continue decreasing.

Critically, the mAP50 curve is still climbing at epoch 100 (ending at ~0.65–0.71) and has not plateaued, suggesting the model had not yet converged when training stopped. With a longer training budget (e.g., 200+ epochs per fold), v3 could likely achieve even higher performance. Val losses also plateau but do not increase, meaning there is no overfitting at this stage.

v4

Training Graphs — Results & Confusion Matrix

YOLO11m · 200 Epochs · RAdam · Batch 32
Training Curves (Losses · Precision · Recall · mAP50)
Normalized Confusion Matrix (Validation)

The training curves tell a stark story of severe underfitting. While train losses (box, cls, dfl) decrease smoothly over 200 epochs, the val losses plateau at a very high level — val/box_loss never goes below ~2.9 and val/cls_loss hovers above 3.0. The large gap between train and val losses confirms the model fails to generalize.

The mAP50 curve only reaches 0.37 after 200 epochs and is still climbing — the model simply needed far more training data than was available for YOLO11m (20M params). Precision oscillates wildly with no stable trend. This version confirms that scaling up the model architecture is counterproductive when the dataset is small.

❌
Crack: 87% miss rate (catastrophic)
87% of crack instances classified as background — the worst result across all versions. YOLO11m has too many parameters to learn crack textures from this limited dataset.
❌
Pothole: 63% miss rate
63% of potholes go undetected — far worse than v3 (38%) or v5 (44%). The combination of large batch size, high workers, and RAdam was ineffective on this dataset.
⚠️
Manhole: 65% recall
Only class with partial success (0.65 recall), but 27% still missed to background. Manholes' distinctive round shapes allow even an undertrained model to partially detect them.
❌
Model still learning at epoch 200
mAP50 did not plateau — confirms YOLO11m requires substantially more data and epochs to converge on this road damage detection task.

Training Graphs — Results & Confusion Matrix

YOLO11s · ~66 Epochs (Early Stop) · AdamW
Training Curves (Losses · Precision · Recall · mAP50)
Normalized Confusion Matrix (Validation)

v5 shows dramatically improved training dynamics versus v4. The switch to YOLO11s and AdamW causes losses to converge much faster — train losses reach stable low values by epoch ~30. Early stopping triggered at ~66 epochs (out of 200 configured), meaning the model stopped learning before the set limit.

However, the val/box_loss begins rising after epoch ~40 (from 2.55 up to ~2.85), and val/cls_loss also trends upward from epoch ~30 — a clear overfitting signal. The mAP50 plateau (~0.43–0.48) reflects this: the model reached its generalization limit with the current augmentation strategy (mixup, copy_paste, rotation).

✅
Manhole: Best recall so far (69%)
69% of manholes correctly detected — the best result to this point. Only 9% missed to background vs 27% in v4, showing the architecture change was crucial.
✅
Pothole: 49% recall (big jump from v4's 29%)
49% correctly detected — significant improvement over v4 (29%). 43% still missed to background, but AdamW + smaller model combination works much better.
⚠️
Crack: 67% miss rate (still problematic)
67% of cracks classified as background — improved from v4's 87%, but cracks remain the hardest class. Only 30% correctly detected.
⚠️
Overfitting after epoch 40
Rising val losses after epoch 40 confirm overfitting. Heavy augmentation (mixup, copy_paste) may have hurt generalization — this is addressed in v6.

v6

Training Graphs — Results & Confusion Matrix

YOLO11s · ~65 Epochs (Patience=20) · AdamW · 960px
Training Curves (Losses · Precision · Recall · mAP50)
Normalized Confusion Matrix (Validation)

The most striking feature is the massive spike in val/cls_loss at epochs 2–3 (reaching ~20.0), followed by a rapid drop to ~4.0 by epoch 10. This is caused by severe class imbalance in early batches — when the first few validation batches contain very few positive examples of one class, AdamW temporarily amplifies the classification error. It resolves on its own and is expected behavior with imbalanced datasets and high initial LR.

After the initial spike, training proceeds cleanly. Train losses decline steadily. Val losses stabilize after epoch ~15 with a slight upward drift in val/box_loss near the end — a milder overfitting signal than v5. The mAP50 ends at ~0.41 with a slight upward trend at cutoff, suggesting patience=20 stopped training slightly before convergence. Despite this, test mAP50 (0.4339) marginally beats v5 (0.4316) — simpler augmentation and higher resolution improved generalization.

⚠️
Crack: 67% miss rate (unchanged from v5)
67% of cracks still classified as background — same as v5. Higher resolution gave a marginal improvement in crack TEST mAP50 (0.260 → 0.263) but did not fundamentally solve the problem.
⚠️
Manhole: 58% recall (declined from v5's 69%)
Manhole recall dropped from 69% (v5) to 58%, with 31% missing to background. The faster early stop (patience=20) likely prevented full learning at 960px.
✅
Pothole: 36% recall (lower than v5's 49%)
Pothole recall dropped (36% vs 49% in v5), but background confusion also dropped — 29% to background vs 44% in v5, suggesting the model is more conservative.
💡
Test performance beats val performance
v6 test mAP50 (0.4339) edges out v5 (0.4316). Removing heavy augmentation (mixup, copy_paste) eliminated noise that was hurting generalization to the test set.

🏆 Conclusion & Verdict

Which model performs best, and why.

🥇 Best Overall Model: v6 (Fairly Evaluated)

Among versions evaluated on a consistent, properly split dataset (75/15/10 group-aware split, 229-image test set), v6 achieves the highest test-set mAP50 of 0.4339 and the best manhole precision of 0.732. It uses YOLO11s (lightweight 10M params) with higher resolution (960px), AdamW optimizer, and a clean, minimal augmentation strategy.

🥈 Runner-up: v5 — Close second (test mAP50: 0.4316)

v5 scores nearly identically to v6 on the test set. It uses more augmentation (mixup=0.1, copy_paste=0.3, degrees=2) which can help generalize but may have slightly hurt the test score.

⚠️ Note on v3: Highest Raw mAP50 (0.711) — not directly comparable

v3 used K-Fold CV and achieved 0.711 mAP50 on a 15-image val set. These are extremely small evaluation sets and should not be compared directly with v4–v6. If v3 is re-evaluated on a larger held-out set, it may prove superior.

📝 Key Lessons Learned Across Versions

✅ What Worked
  • Group-aware train/val/test split
  • AdamW outperforms RAdam at this scale
  • YOLO11s outperforms YOLO11m on small datasets
  • Higher resolution (896→960px) improved quality
  • Auto-batch (0.8) maximizes GPU utilization
  • Minimal augmentation > heavy augmentation here
❌ What Didn't Work
  • Train=Val setup (v1): inflated metrics
  • Scaling to YOLO11m (v4): much worse results
  • Heavy augmentation hurt v4/v5
  • RAdam + high momentum (v4): suboptimal convergence
  • Large batch size (32, v4): potential underfitting
🚀 Recommended Next Steps
  • Re-evaluate v3 K-Fold on a larger holdout set
  • Try YOLO11s-p2 for better crack detection
  • Optuna LR tuning on full dataset
  • Weighted sampling for crack class imbalance
  • Combine v3 K-Fold + v6 hyperparameters
